# User Settings Hub

Refference attached screen recording se e ek kam karo ki jaise refference screen recording hain. Usi ka use karke same to same a-z esa hi ho ek settings ka esa page banao but isme main important yah hain ki isme " Currency , Language Me ( hindi or english) ho baki language na ho. Apperance Bhi ho and andar " Mono , Midnight , Warm " ho.  But isme " Region " bhi ho jisme ki " India " , " Outside India " ho bas. And " Midnight Navigation"  ho jisme ki " Drift " , Limelight " , Floting " ho. Preference" ho jisme ki " Show Price " , " Reduce Motion " , " Ongoing Story Updates " yah ho inme on/off toggle ho ..  and " Sound " ho jisme ki " Payment success sound " , " Episode delivery sound " , " Sound Effects " , " Demo request sound " ho on/off ho.   And isme Jo logout hain uski jagah " Reset onboarding " ho.   Isme " Network diagnostics " , " Permissions " na ho jo ki refference video me hain. Yah mobile first hona chahiye.
